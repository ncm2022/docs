All the universal features of myIMS are listed here.

### **Notifications, Notice Board, Calendar**

On the Home Page, you will be able to access the following features:

- **Notifications:** A sliding panel that notifies you about important events.
- **Notice Board:** A list of upcoming sessions, events, and tests.
- **Calendar:** A list of upcoming sessions, events, and tests.
- **Blogs:** A collection of important blogs to support your test preparation journey.

### **Prepare Tab**

This is crucial for your study journey. It consists of three sections listed below.

1. Guide - Everything you need for entrance exams.
2. Module - Refer to organized sections of learning resources and study material categorized by subject, topic, or exam section. Each module typically contains a structured set of content designed to help students understand concepts, practice questions, and assess their performance.
3. GK - Stay updated with our General Knowledge section! Make the most of these resources. Your bright future is just around the corner!

### **MyPage Tab**

myPage consists of three sections:

1. Bookmark - This section displays all the items you have bookmarked.
2. MyTask - Here, you will find anything you’ve added to your tasks.
3. MyPlan - This section includes one-on-one mentorship sessions with IMS mentors. During these sessions, you can discuss creating a study plan, analysing mock exams, improving your scores, and strategising for college applications and selections. The number of myPlan sessions you receive will depend on the program you are enrolled in.

### **Performance Tab**

The Performance Tab is designed to provide detailed insights into your progress during exam preparation. It tracks your performance across various tests, sections, and topics, allowing you to identify your strengths and weaknesses.
