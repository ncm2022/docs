### **Benchmarking Tests**

**Benchmarking Tests:** When you are confident that your conceptual understanding has improved, it’s time for a Benchmarking Test. Depending on your performance in the benchmarking test, you will receive one of the four titles:

- Learner \<80%
- Capable 80 to 95 %
- Advanced 95 to 99 %
- Ninja 99 \+

You are ready to move to the next topic once you reach a Capable level on a topic.

Access Path - _Log in to [myIMS 3.0](https://myimsv3.imsindia.com/) \> Prepare \> Modules \> Fundamentals of QA-VA \> Quantitative Ability/ Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension \> Benchmarking Test \> Take the Test_

### **Module Test**

After completion of each Module, there is a module test that evaluates students’ understanding of concepts across all subjects covered in that specific module. In all, the syllabus is broken into 5 modules, and accordingly, you will have to take 5 Module tests of the BBA level entrance test & 4 Module tests of the IPMAT Indore level.

Access Path - _Log in to [myIMS 3.0](https://myimsv3.imsindia.com/) \> Test Tab \>_ Modules \> Fundamental Module Test \> Take the Test

### **In-Class Tests**

The in-class tests are designed to assess whether students have grasped the fundamental concepts taught during lessons and to ensure they have completed the Practice Exercises.

Access Path - _Login to [myIMS 3.0](https://myimsv3.imsindia.com/) \> Modules tab \> Fundamentals of QA-VA \>_ In-class test is available with every section
