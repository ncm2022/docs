myPlans are one-on-one Mentorship sessions with IMS mentors. Based on the program enrolled, you would get a specific number of one-on-one myPlan sessions

**Go to**  [myIMS portal](https://myimsv3.imsindia.com/) **\> myPage \> myPlan \> Book A New Session**

If you’ve booked a Virtual myPlan session, a **‘Join Now’** button will appear in the **myPlan tab** five minutes before the session begins.

Simply click on the **‘Join Now’** button to access the session.

**Note:** If you see a message asking you to wait for admission, it means you are not using your **IMS-registered email ID** to join. Please ensure you always use your registered email ID to join these sessions.

**Typically, the following are the topics for discussion**

- **PTM - Goal Setting & IMS resources**
  - This myPLAN should be completed within the first 15 days of admission. You should discuss your dream college/specialisation and gauge how much effort you will need to achieve the desired outcome.
- **Study Plan**
  - This myPLAN should be taken during the Fundamental Module and after the first three modules are over for a midway check. The objective of this myPLAN is to check if you are on track and what action needs to be taken to bring you back on track. You can also discuss how you should study and your weekly study schedule.
- **SimTest Analysis**
  - Once you have attempted at least two SimTests, you should use this myPLAN. Here, you can discuss how to improve your scores, time management, or any other challenges you might be facing in SimTest.
- **PTM - College Application Strategy**
  - You should discuss applying to various colleges and appearing for various exams based on your scores in the SimTest. The idea is to have a few ambitious colleges, a few realistic, and a few as a backup.
- **Post Board Prep Strategy**
  - Once the board exams are over, you should discuss the study plan for the remaining time, depending upon your preparedness, so that you can maximise your score in the limited time after the board exams.
- **PTM - Progress Check**
  - Your performance in the Benchmarking test and Module test will be discussed, and accordingly, a study plan will be suggested.
